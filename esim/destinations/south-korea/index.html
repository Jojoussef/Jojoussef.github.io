Hack #2 · Duration Inversion

Where buying an extra day costs less

We found 4 duration inversions in South Korea. Each row means the longer plan is genuinely cheaper than the shorter one.

CurrencyShorter planLonger plan (cheaper)Instant saving

GBP 57d = £83.00 58d = £79.00 -£4.00 (4.8%)
GBP 39d = £70.00 40d = £68.00 -£2.00 (2.9%)
GBP 44d = £72.00 45d = £71.00 -£1.00 (1.4%)
Why this happens: the vendor prices its catalogue off a single Euro base and re-rounds into each local currency. When the rounding lands badly, a longer plan can land under a shorter one. It is a pricing artefact, not a promotion — and it disappears the moment the catalogue is re-priced, so grab it while the table looks like this.
