Methodology

Everything on this site comes from one static catalogue snapshot, cross-checked against public reference rates. No estimates, no scraped competitor pages, no models.

1. The catalogue snapshot

The vendor publishes a per-plan price table for every destination, in 20 currencies, across every duration they sell. We captured the full table on 2026-10-15: 94 destinations, 6,802 plan variants, 136,040 individual price points.

2. FX normalisation

Every price is converted to USD using the 2026-10-15 ECB euro reference rate and IBAN rates. The rate table is published in full on the FX audit page, so every conversion on this site can be re-checked by hand.

3. Inversion detection

For every destination and currency we sort the durations ascending and flag any adjacent pair where the longer plan's total price is lower than the shorter one's. We found 224 such pairs plus 80 duplicate rows where the same currency key carries two conflicting prices.

4. iOS visibility

The vendor's iOS app does not list every duration the web checkout sells. We diff the two catalogues: 4,509 of 6,802 plan variants (66.3%) are web-only. Those durations are often where the inversions hide.

5. Competitor benchmarks

For 7 destinations we recorded the published list price of competing eSIM products (Nomad, Saily, Airalo, Yesim, Jetpac) so the calculator can show a genuine break-even rather than a brand ranking. Benchmarks are 2026-10 list prices and move often.

6. How this site earns money

Through affiliate links. We earn a commission when you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. Ranking is never paid for. The "cheapest provider" card is sorted by price, and a paid placement would have to be labelled as such. Full details on the disclosure page.
