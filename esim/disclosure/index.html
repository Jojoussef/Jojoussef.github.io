Prices come from the vendor's own published catalogue, captured on 2026-10-15, and converted to USD using the ECB euro reference rate and IBAN rates. We do not receive special pricing, hidden discounts or preferential treatment. If our affiliate relationship ever did change a price, we would not show that price.
