Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-15

The short version

This site has no accounts, no login, no database and no server-side collection. We do not know who you are and we do not build a profile of you.

What we collect

Nothing directly. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your selections (destination, duration, currency) are held in page memory and are never transmitted anywhere. We deliberately removed the tool's local-storage save feature from the public deployment so that no behavioural data accumulates in your browser either.

Third parties

Two categories of third party are involved:

  • Content delivery: Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, Google Fonts and the Inter typeface are loaded from CDNs. Those providers will see your IP address as part of serving the file.
  • Affiliate links: when you click a "buy" button you are sent to a vendor (Holafly, Nomad, Saily, Airalo, Yesim, Jetpac or Cellesim) and their own privacy policy applies from that point on. We receive a referral notification from them; we do not receive your personal data.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. The affiliate links carry UTM parameters in the query string; these are read by the destination vendor, not stored here.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, standard technical logs (IP address, user agent, requested path) may be processed for security and abuse prevention, in line with the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the public issue tracker on the source repository.
