224 places where buying an extra day costs less

A duration inversion is a pricing bug in the vendor catalogue: the longer plan is listed at a lower total price than the shorter one. These are real, live rows from the 2026-10-15 catalogue, converted for readability.

Biggest one right now: in Dominican Republic (DOM), the 26-day plan costs COL$336,999.00 while the 25-day plan costs COL$426,999.00. That is COL$90,000.00 cheaper (21.1%) for more coverage.
